What it is. Each traced stroke is a closed walk of unit teeth on the Eisenstein lattice. The tones are curlicues: Gauss walks with quadratic phase that close exactly by the block-rotation identity. The cyan arrows are the comb window, the last m teeth before the pen tip; the amber chord is their exact resultant. Each tone tile is turned by an integer power of the root of unity ζ₁₀₀₈₀ (rot + 1009k + 2017l at lattice point (k, l)); rotating a tile multiplies it by a root of unity, so the block identity and the closure certificate do not depend on the rotation (checked here for the actual rotations).

Why it matters. Closure is certified in exact integer arithmetic, the data is integers only, and the file is small.

Credits. Drawing: saphir. Instrument: mcomb. Curlicue closure identity and program: Opus (Claude Opus 5.5).

Limits. The tracing is numerical (a threshold and five tone levels). The bridges are tiny but present. The light area under the belly is enclosed paper, part of the outline region.

Tracing: numerical (from the drawing). Teeth, vertices, closure, curlicues: exact integers in ℤ[ζ₆] and ℤ[ζ₁₀₀₈₀]. Drawing: floating point (drawing register).
