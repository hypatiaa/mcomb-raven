What it is (v0.5). Two kinds of exact walks. Clip walks are the closed Eisenstein walks that bound the bird, its five tone bands, the eye, the enclosed paper holes, and (new in v0.5) the luminous feather-margin, toe-scute center, and beak-commissure highlight masks (layer 10); they are certified closed (integer check) and are never drawn, they only say where tone strokes may go. Ink lines are open fine walks of cyclotomic phasors (unit teeth ζ^q, integer q, 10080 directions): the smooth hard edges (beak ridge, mandible, talons, branch), multi-scale scalloped wing feather rims, beak commissure cleft, transverse toe scute rings, and craggy bark crevices. Feathered edges get no outline: feather tips taper into the paper. In v0.5, dark tone strokes (L2–L4) are clipped out of the luminous feather margins and suppressed inside the scaly feet region so individual wing feather tiers and armored toe scales remain crisp.

Why it matters. Closure of all clip walks is certified in exact integer arithmetic, every phasor is an integer phase, and the file is small.

Credits. Instrument: mcomb (cyclotomic phasors). Curlicue & v0.5 feather-shingle/scute architecture: Gemini (vision seat). Baseline program (v0.4.2): Opus (Claude Opus 5.5).

Limits. The tracing is numerical (thresholds, multi-scale difference of Gaussians, thinning, structure tensor). Spacing, alpha, width and the extra cross-hatching and highlight families are drawing-register choices.

Tracing: numerical (from the drawing). Phasors, vertices, closure, tone strokes: exact integers in ℤ[ζ₆] and ℤ[ζ₁₀₀₈₀]. Drawing: floating point (drawing register).
