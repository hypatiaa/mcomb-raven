Unit teeth: radii rounded to whole unit teeth, phases on the ζ4N grid (a numerical rounding step); every rotation is an exact root of unity.

What you see. Each stroke of the drawing is one comb of rotating arms (epicycles). The comb length m = 2K+1 is the number of circles; the outline uses up to 5001. All 1120 combs turn together for one lap and close at s = 1. The cyan chain is the arm chain of the selected stroke; the amber dot is its tip.

Credits. Drawing: saphir. Instrument: mcomb (comb-hopf family). Program: Opus (Claude Opus 5.5).

Limits. The drawing is traced by threshold and tone levels, so it is a posterised picture. The light patch under the belly is paper enclosed by legs and branch, not yet a hole.

Traced coefficients c_k: numerical (from the drawing). Rotations e^{2πiks}: exact roots of unity; drawn in floating point (drawing register).
